# DIGITAL MARKETING WEBSITE — PREMIUM BLUE & WHITE EXPERIENCE

## 1. PROJECT GOAL

Build a **production-ready, premium digital marketing agency website** with a strong visual identity and an impressive first-time user experience.

The website should feel:

* Modern
* Premium
* Cinematic
* Creative
* Technology-driven
* Professional
* High-converting
* Visually memorable

This is **not a basic corporate website**.

The design should feel like a high-end digital marketing + creative media agency that understands branding, content, technology and modern web experiences.

---

# 2. MOST IMPORTANT RULE — HERO SECTION

The **Hero Section is the most important section of the entire website.**

The user should immediately understand:

> "This is a digital marketing and media growth company."

The hero must create a strong visual impression within the first few seconds.

## Hero Background

I have already created a **background video** for the Hero Section.

Use the existing video asset from the project's `assets` folder.

### Video requirements

* Use the video as the full-screen hero background.
* Do NOT replace the video with an image.
* Do NOT place the video inside a small card.
* Video should cover the complete hero viewport.
* Use `object-fit: cover`.
* Maintain the video's visual quality.
* Video should autoplay.
* Video should loop continuously.
* Video should be muted.
* Use `playsInline`.
* Do not show browser video controls.
* Optimize loading so the page does not feel slow.

### Video readability

The background video is visually important, but the **marketing hook must remain clearly readable while the video is playing**.

Do NOT allow bright/dark areas of the video to destroy text readability.

Use an intelligent overlay system:

* subtle blue-tinted overlay
* soft white-to-transparent gradient where required
* subtle blue vignette
* contrast adjustment if necessary

Do not make the overlay so strong that the video becomes invisible.

The video should remain visible and cinematic.

# 19. COLOR BALANCE

The website must clearly look **BLUE + WHITE**, not black + blue.

Use the colors approximately as a visual direction:

* 45% white / very light surfaces
* 30% deep blue / navy
* 15% blue gradients
* 10% bright blue accents

These are design guidelines, not strict CSS percentages.

The final design should feel:

**Clean + Premium + Blue + White + Modern + High-Tech**

Avoid excessive darkness.
